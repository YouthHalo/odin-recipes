Pizza Hut Pizza at home

Description

A copy of Pizza from Pizza Hut

Ingredients

  • Cell Phone
  • Mode of transportation

Steps

  1. Call your local pizza hut
  2. Order your preferred pizza size and toppings
  3. Use mode of transportation to arrive at the Pizza Hut
  4. Wait for your pizza to finish cooking
  5. Pick up your pizza
  6. Drive back home
  7. Enjoy!
